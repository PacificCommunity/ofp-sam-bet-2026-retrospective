\paragraph{Recent fishing-mortality diagnostic.}
For a model fitted through year $b$, the value evaluated at year $t$ is
\[
\overline{F}^{(b)}_{t}=\frac{1}{4}\sum_{y=t-4}^{t-1}F^{(b)}_{y}.
\]
For peel $p$, $t=T-p$ and
\[
d_{F,p}=\frac{\overline{F}^{(T-p)}_{T-p}-\overline{F}^{(T)}_{T-p}}{\overline{F}^{(T)}_{T-p}},\qquad
\rho_F=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{p=1}^{n}d_{F,p}.
\]
For the 7 converged peels, $\rho_F=0.231531$.
Annual instantaneous fishing mortality is averaged over 4 years from $T-4$ to $T-1$.
